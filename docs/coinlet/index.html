Coinlet

Track it. Budget it. Save it.

Coinlet is a friendly, private money tracker for students and young earners. Log pocket money, part-time income and everyday spending, set budgets, and save up for the things you want.

  • Works 100% offline: the app has no internet permission
  • No online account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Budgets, savings goals, repeating bills, accounts and debts
  • Optional PIN and fingerprint login, stored only on your phone
  • English and हिन्दी
