Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

APP NAME ("the app") is a personal expense and budget tracker developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

APP NAME does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • Transactions (amount, type, category, date and optional note)
  • Categories, budgets, savings goals, accounts, repeating entries and debts you record
  • Your profile: the name you choose and a login PIN. The PIN is never stored as-is, only as a salted, one-way hash, and it never leaves the phone
  • App settings (currency, theme, reminder time, fingerprint-login preference)

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

  • No online account is needed. The in-app login (name + PIN) is a local profile that exists only on your device.
  • The app contains no advertising.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

Backups and exports

  • Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app.
  • You can choose to export your data to a CSV file or a JSON backup file. These files are saved only where you choose through the Android file picker (for example your device storage or a cloud drive app you use). Once exported, these files are under your control and subject to the privacy practices of wherever you store or share them.
  • When you restore a backup, the app reads only the file you select.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show the optional daily reminder you turn on. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Biometric: used only if you turn on "Log in with fingerprint". Your fingerprint or face data is handled entirely by Android; the app never has access to it.
  • Run at startup and keep awake: used only so the daily reminder and repeating entries still run on time after the phone restarts. They do not access any of your data outside the app.

Children's privacy

APP NAME is suitable for teenagers and young adults. Because the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children, no personal data of children is collected or shared.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data at any time from Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes all app data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Because the app collects no data, any change would only ever describe new on-device features.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email pratham.supportt@gmail.com.
